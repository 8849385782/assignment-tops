What changed, and why

BeforeAfter
font-weight400500
letter-spacing-0.01em (cramped)0.01em (open)
line-height1.1 (dense)1.75 (airy)

Tighter line-height and negative letter-spacing make long paragraphs feel like a wall of text; opening both up gives the eye clear "landing points" per line.
